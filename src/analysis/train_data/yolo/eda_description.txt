An exploratory analysis of the CV object-detection datasets was conducted prior to YOLO model training.
The dataset contains 3497 images, of which 3351 are valid annotated samples.

Overall, 98.31% of the CVs contain at least one detectable visual element.
Photographs are present in 46.93% of the resumes, while skill tables appear in
32.37% of the documents.

On average, each CV includes 7.46 text regions, 0.47 photographs,
and 1.95 skill tables. The median aspect ratio of the images is 1.0.

Color distribution analysis shows a mean colorfulness of 17.346 and an average color
variation of 0.2, indicating moderate visual diversity with consistent document structure.

These findings confirm that the dataset provides sufficient structural variability and annotation
coverage to support robust YOLO-based layout detection in curriculum vitae documents.
